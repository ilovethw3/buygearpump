The three-screw insulation pump is mainly used for high-viscosity lubricating liquids that cannot be conveyed at normal temperature. It is commonly used for asphalt, heavy fuel oil, crude oil, and similar media in transfer and burner supply applications.

The pump is designed with heat preservation support. The heat carrier can be steam, thermal oil, hot water, or other suitable heating medium according to the operating requirements. The general maximum working temperature is ≤250°C. If the required operating temperature exceeds this range, it should be specified when ordering.

The pump uses a modular component design and is available in multiple structural forms, including baseplate-mounted, bracket-mounted, and foot-mounted configurations. It can be used for horizontal, flange-mounted, or vertical installation, and the inlet and outlet directions can be arranged on the left or right side according to system requirements.

## Features & Advantages

- **Designed for Heated Media:** Suitable for high-viscosity lubricating liquids that require heat preservation before transfer.
- **Wide Flow Range:** Flow range is 1.6–600 m³/h according to the source material.
- **High Working Pressure:** Maximum working pressure can reach 4.0 MPa.
- **Flexible Structure:** Available in baseplate, bracket, and foot-mounted designs.
- **Multiple Installation Options:** Supports horizontal, flange-mounted, and vertical installation.
- **Adjustable Port Direction:** Pump inlet and outlet directions can be arranged on the left or right side.
- **Heat Carrier Options:** Steam, thermal oil, hot water, and other heating media can be used as heat carriers.

## Typical Applications

The three-screw insulation pump is commonly used in systems that require heated transfer of high-viscosity lubricating liquids.

Typical applications include:

- Asphalt transfer
- Heavy fuel oil transfer
- Crude oil transfer
- Burner supply systems
- Fuel supply and transfer in heating equipment
- Hydraulic and lubrication systems in machinery
- Loading, transfer, and liquid supply in chemical, petrochemical, and food industries
- Marine transfer, boosting, fuel injection, lubricating oil, and marine hydraulic systems

## Suitable Media

The three-screw insulation pump is suitable for the following types of media:

- **Lubricating Liquids:** Mechanical oil, lubricating oil, heavy oil, and residual oil.
- **Low-Lubricity Liquids:** Light diesel oil, heavy diesel oil, and wax-containing thin oil.
- **Viscous Liquids:** Synthetic rubber liquid, artificial rubber liquid, and emulsions.
