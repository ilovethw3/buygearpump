The RY stainless steel heat transfer oil pump is designed for conveying high-temperature liquids without solid particles. It is suitable for thermal oil circulation and high-temperature liquid transfer systems where stable operation and reliable heat dissipation are required.

The pump is available with different main wetted-part material options according to the medium and operating temperature requirements. The source material lists three material categories: HT200, ZG230-450, and 1Cr18Ni9Ti / 0Cr18Ni12Mo2Ti.

The RY stainless steel heat transfer oil pump adopts a self-cooling structure, replacing the traditional water-cooling design. This helps simplify the pump structure, reduce overall size, lower operating cost, and support stable operation in thermal oil service.

The pump uses a combination of packing seal and mechanical seal. The packing seal is selected for high-temperature adaptability according to actual operating conditions. The product is mainly used for weakly corrosive high-temperature liquids without solid particles. The source material lists a maximum service temperature of ≤370°C, while the design-point performance range lists a temperature range of -20°C to 350°C.

## Features & Advantages

- **Designed for Thermal Oil Service:** Suitable for high-temperature liquid transfer without solid particles.
- **Self-Cooling Design:** Eliminates the traditional water-cooling structure for a simpler and more compact pump layout.
- **Stable Operation:** Designed for continuous heat transfer oil circulation and industrial high-temperature liquid service.
- **Material Options:** Main wetted-part material options include HT200, ZG230-450, and stainless steel grades listed in the source material.
- **Combined Sealing Structure:** Uses packing seal and mechanical seal together for supported operating conditions.
- **Wide Performance Coverage:** Flow, head, and temperature ranges support multiple thermal oil system requirements.

## Typical Applications

The RY stainless steel heat transfer oil pump is suitable for:

- Heat transfer oil circulation systems
- Thermal oil heating systems
- High-temperature liquid transfer
- Weakly corrosive high-temperature liquid transfer
- Industrial heating and temperature-control systems
