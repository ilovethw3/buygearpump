The WCB Mini Portable Handheld Gear Pump is a low-pressure, energy-saving oil transfer pump designed for convenient mobile use. It is suitable for transferring clean neutral oils from one container to another, including engine oil, kerosene, diesel, mineral oil, and various vegetable oils.

This portable gear pump features a compact body, light weight, self-priming operation, and a carrying handle, making it easy for users to move and operate by hand. According to the source description, it can be started with 220V power and is suitable for oil drum transfer, oil depot transfer, and thin oil lubrication system service.

The pump is mainly composed of gears, shafts, pump body, and shaft-end sealing components. The liquid being transferred must not contain solid particles or fibers. It is intended for clean, non-corrosive liquids or liquids with properties similar to lubricating oil.

## Features & Advantages

- **Mini Portable Structure:** Small size and light weight make the pump convenient for mobile oil transfer.
- **Handheld Design:** Equipped with a handle for easy carrying and on-site operation.
- **Self-Priming Performance:** Helps transfer oil efficiently between containers.
- **Low-Pressure Oil Transfer:** Designed as a micro portable energy-saving oil transfer pump.
- **Clean Oil Compatibility:** Suitable for lubricating oil, engine oil, kerosene, diesel, mineral oil, and vegetable oil.
- **Improved Work Efficiency:** Helps reduce manual labor during oil transfer operations.

## Typical Applications

- Oil drum transfer where fixed power equipment is not available
- Oil transfer in refineries, power plants, substations, and oil depots
- Thin oil lubrication systems
- Commercial, industrial, and agricultural oil transfer
- Transfer of clean neutral oils from one container to another
