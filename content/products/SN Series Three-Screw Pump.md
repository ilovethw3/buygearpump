The SN series three-screw pump is designed for conveying oils and other liquids with lubricating properties. With a modular and standardized component design, the pump can be configured for horizontal, flange-mounted, or vertical installation.

According to the requirements of the conveyed medium, the SN series three-screw pump can also be supplied with heating or cooling structures. This allows the pump to support different operating conditions for oil transfer, fuel supply, hydraulic, lubrication, loading, and liquid supply systems.

The pump uses a hydraulically balanced rotor design, helping reduce vibration and noise during operation. It provides stable output with low pulsation, good efficiency, and strong self-priming capability. Its compact structure, small size, and light weight also support operation at relatively high speed.

## Features & Advantages

- **Hydraulically Balanced Rotor:** Helps reduce vibration and operating noise.
- **Stable Flow Output:** Delivers liquid smoothly with low pulsation.
- **Good Efficiency:** Designed for efficient transfer of oils and lubricating liquids.
- **Strong Self-Priming Capability:** Supports reliable suction performance in suitable systems.
- **Modular Component Design:** Standardized parts allow multiple installation configurations.
- **Compact Structure:** Small size, light weight, and suitable for relatively high-speed operation.

## Typical Applications

The SN series three-screw pump is used in heating equipment, marine systems, chemical and petrochemical processing, food processing, and machinery industries.

Common applications include:

- **Heating Equipment:** Fuel injection, fuel supply, and fuel transfer pump.
- **Marine Systems:** Transfer pump, booster pump, fuel injection pump, lubricating oil pump, and marine hydraulic equipment pump.
- **Chemical, Petrochemical & Food Industries:** Loading pump, transfer pump, and liquid supply pump.
- **Machinery Industry:** Hydraulic pump, lubrication pump, and remote-control motor pump.

## Suitable Media

The SN series three-screw pump is suitable for the following types of media:

- **Lubricating Liquids:** Mechanical oil, lubricating oil, heavy oil, and residual oil.
- **Low-Lubricity Liquids:** Light diesel oil, heavy diesel oil, and wax-containing thin oil.
- **Viscous Liquids:** Synthetic rubber liquid, artificial rubber liquid, and emulsions.
