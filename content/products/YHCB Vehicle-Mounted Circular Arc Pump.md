The YHCB vehicle-mounted circular arc pump is mainly suitable for transferring clean, non-corrosive oil media without solid particles. The applicable medium viscosity is from 5 to 1500 cSt, and the medium temperature should be below 80°C.

The pump can be installed on a vehicle and driven by the power take-off output shaft of the vehicle gearbox. It is suitable for mobile oil transfer applications where a truck-mounted pump structure is required.

## Structure & Configuration

This series is available with three gear configurations: nylon gear, steel gear, and copper gear. Nylon gears and copper gears are suitable for transferring gasoline, kerosene, diesel, and similar flammable or explosive media when properly configured for the operating conditions.

For applications such as beverage transfer or chemical raw material transfer, the pump can be customized according to user requirements and the actual characteristics of the medium.

The circular arc gear pump can also be mounted on a movable trolley for mobile installation and use. This requirement should be specified when ordering.

## Features & Advantages

- **Truck-Mounted Operation:** Can be driven by the vehicle gearbox power take-off output shaft.
- **Mobile Oil Transfer:** Suitable for vehicle-mounted or trolley-mounted transfer applications.
- **Circular Arc Gear Design:** Designed for stable oil transfer service.
- **Multiple Gear Materials:** Nylon, steel, and copper gear options are available according to the medium.
- **Fuel Transfer Use:** Nylon and copper gear configurations can be used for gasoline, kerosene, diesel, and similar media under suitable conditions.
- **Customizable Configuration:** Can be customized for beverage, chemical raw material, or other special media transfer requirements.

## Typical Applications

- Vehicle-mounted oil transfer
- Mobile fuel transfer
- Gasoline transfer with suitable configuration
- Kerosene transfer
- Diesel transfer
- Truck-mounted pump systems
- Movable trolley-mounted pump systems
- Customized beverage or chemical raw material transfer applications
