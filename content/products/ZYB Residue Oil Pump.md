The ZYB residue oil pump is suitable for transferring heavy oil, diesel oil, coal tar, and other media containing non-metallic impurities. It can also be used in grain and oil processing, chemical, building materials, and daily chemical production industries for transferring various media with impurities and low lubricity.

This pump is suitable for media with temperature not higher than 200°C and viscosity not greater than 150 mm²/s. It is especially suitable for applications requiring stable working pressure and long-term continuous operation, such as lubrication systems and fuel delivery or injection systems.

The ZYB residue oil pump is not suitable for corrosive media, media containing metallic impurities, sand or stone impurities with particle diameter greater than 3 mm, highly volatile media, or media with a low flash point.

## Structure & Working Features

The ZYB residue oil pump adopts a self-sealing lubrication structure and an automatic clearance adjustment structure. Key components are made of stable-performance materials as described in the source. The pump features a compact structure, good self-priming ability, good efficiency, and strong adaptability.

For applications where the transferred liquid contains impurities, the service life of this pump is described as longer than that of ordinary gear pumps. The design also considers interchangeability with the KCB series standard gear pump. Its external dimensions, installation dimensions, and technical parameters are aligned with the KCB series gear pump.

When transferring lubricating media without impurities, the pump also supports long service life and stable pressure performance.

## Features & Advantages

- **Handles Non-Metallic Impurities:** Suitable for heavy oil, diesel oil, coal tar, and similar media containing non-metallic impurities.
- **Stable Continuous Operation:** Designed for working conditions requiring stable pressure and long-term continuous operation.
- **Self-Sealing Lubrication:** Internal structure supports reliable lubrication during operation.
- **Automatic Clearance Adjustment:** Helps improve adaptability when transferring media with impurities.
- **Compact Pump Design:** Compact structure with good self-priming performance.
- **KCB Series Interchangeability:** External and installation dimensions are designed to match KCB series gear pumps.
