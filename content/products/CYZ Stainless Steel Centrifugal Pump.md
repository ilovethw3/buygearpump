The CYZ stainless steel centrifugal pump is designed for transferring petroleum products such as gasoline, kerosene, diesel, and aviation kerosene. It is suitable for media temperatures from -20°C to 80°C and can be used as a marine oil loading and unloading pump.

This pump is also suitable for oil transfer in land oil depots, tank trucks, and other oil storage systems. According to the source material, it can also be used to transfer seawater, fresh water, and other suitable liquids.

The CYZ stainless steel centrifugal pump has a simple structure, convenient operation and maintenance, stable running performance, large capacity, good efficiency, easy adjustment, and a wide application range.

The pump adopts an axial liquid return pump body structure. The pump body consists of a suction chamber, liquid storage chamber, volute chamber, return hole, and gas-liquid separation chamber. The bearing uses a single-row radial ball bearing. Some pump models are designed with a cooling chamber at the bottom of the bearing housing. A sealing ring is arranged between the high-pressure and low-pressure areas inside the pump, and the shaft end uses a mechanical seal.
