The CB heavy oil pump is a horizontal positive displacement rotary pump designed for transferring lubricating viscous liquids. It is suitable for media such as heavy oil, industrial light oil, thermal oil, vegetable oil, paint, dye, and similar fluids, with medium temperature not higher than 260°C.

This pump is not suitable for highly corrosive liquids, liquids containing hard particles or fibers, highly volatile media, or low-flash-point liquids such as ether, gasoline, and benzene.

## Structure & Working Features

The CB heavy oil pump uses a horizontal positive displacement rotary structure. It is designed for stable transfer of viscous liquids with lubricating properties. The pump features a simple structure and is convenient to inspect, maintain, and service during regular operation.

## Features & Advantages

- **Suitable for Lubricating Viscous Media:** Designed for heavy oil, industrial light oil, thermal oil, vegetable oil, paint, dye, and similar liquids.
- **Horizontal Positive Displacement Pump:** Rotary pump structure supports steady transfer of viscous media.
- **High-Temperature Media Handling:** Suitable for media temperatures up to 260°C.
- **Simple Structure:** Easy to use, inspect, and maintain.
- **Broad Industrial Use:** Applicable to machinery, petroleum, chemical, metallurgy, textile, pharmaceutical, food, paper, and light industry systems.
- **Clear Media Limits:** Not suitable for highly corrosive, particle-containing, fibrous, highly volatile, or low-flash-point liquids.

## Typical Applications

- Heavy oil transfer
- Industrial light oil transfer
- Thermal oil circulation or transfer
- Vegetable oil transfer
- Paint and dye transfer
- Petroleum and chemical process transfer
- Textile, pharmaceutical, food, paper, and light industry fluid handling
