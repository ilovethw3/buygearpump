The LYB Vertical Circular Arc Gear Pump is a vertical gear pump designed for forced lubrication and long-term continuous operation in thin oil station systems. It is widely used in large mechanical equipment where stable oil delivery, low noise, and reliable service life are important.

Unlike ordinary involute gear pumps, the LYB pump uses a double circular arc sinusoidal gear profile. During gear meshing, the tooth profiles have no relative sliding, which helps reduce tooth surface wear and eliminates trapped oil. This design supports smooth operation, lower noise, longer service life, and improved efficiency.

The pump is mainly composed of gears, shafts, pump body, pump cover, bearing sleeves, and shaft-end sealing components. This series is supplied with a mechanical seal as standard, and packing seal can also be provided according to user requirements.

## Features & Advantages

- **Double Circular Arc Gear Profile:** Uses a sinusoidal circular arc tooth form instead of a conventional involute gear design.
- **Reduced Tooth Wear:** No relative sliding between tooth profiles during meshing helps minimize gear surface wear.
- **No Trapped Oil Phenomenon:** The gear design helps avoid oil trapping during operation.
- **Stable and Quiet Operation:** Designed for smooth running with low noise.
- **Long Service Life:** Reduced wear and stable meshing support extended operating life.
- **Flexible Seal Options:** Mechanical seal is standard, with packing seal available upon request.

## Typical Applications

- Forced lubrication systems for compressors and refrigeration machines
- Marine equipment lubrication systems
- Thin oil station systems in large mechanical equipment
- Petroleum, chemical, steel, metallurgy, mining, power, and environmental protection industries
- Long-term continuous lubrication service in heavy-duty industrial equipment
