The F Series stainless steel gear pump is designed for transferring corrosive liquids or liquids that require higher sanitary conditions. It is suitable for media with temperatures up to 200°C and viscosity from 5 to 1500 cSt, including acids, alkalis, various chemical raw materials, and food-related liquids.

This pump features a compact structure, stable performance, and a clean exterior design. Its stainless steel construction makes it suitable for demanding transfer applications where corrosion resistance and reliable operation are important.

The pump uses a pair of meshing gears mounted on a driving shaft and a driven shaft inside the pump housing. As the gears rotate, liquid enters through the inlet, is carried around the gear cavity, and is discharged through the outlet. According to the source description, ceramic plates are installed on the inner wall of the cavity that contains the gear pair, helping improve acid resistance and performance stability.

## Features & Advantages

- **Stainless Steel Gear Pump Design:** Built for transferring corrosive or sanitary-process liquids.
- **Suitable for Multiple Media:** Applicable to acids, alkalis, chemical raw materials, and food-related liquids.
- **Stable Gear Transmission:** A driving gear and driven gear form a meshing gear pair for consistent liquid transfer.
- **Ceramic-Lined Inner Cavity:** Ceramic plates inside the gear cavity help improve acid resistance.
- **Compact Structure:** Designed with a compact body and stable operating performance.
- **Wide Viscosity Range:** Suitable for liquids with viscosity from 5 to 1500 cSt.

## Typical Applications

- Transfer of acidic and alkaline liquids
- Chemical raw material delivery
- Food-related liquid transfer where higher sanitary conditions are required
- Handling of corrosive liquids with temperatures up to 200°C
