The ZYB-Z heavy oil one-to-one pump is designed for transferring heavy oil and similar media under stable-pressure, continuous-duty working conditions. It is suitable for heavy oil, diesel oil, coal tar, and various low-lubricity media containing non-metallic impurities.

This pump is suitable for media with temperature not higher than 200°C and viscosity not greater than 150 mm²/s. It can be used in grain and oil processing, chemical, building materials, daily chemical production, fuel delivery, and lubrication-related systems where the transferred medium may contain impurities and has limited lubricity.

The pump is especially suitable for applications requiring stable working pressure and long-term continuous operation, such as lubrication systems and fuel delivery or injection systems.

The ZYB-Z pump is not suitable for corrosive media, media containing metallic impurities, sand or stone impurities with particle diameter greater than 3 mm, highly volatile media, or low-flash-point liquids such as gasoline and benzene.

## Structure & Working Features

The ZYB-Z heavy oil one-to-one pump adopts a self-sealing lubrication structure and an automatic clearance adjustment structure. Key components are made of stable-performance materials as described in the source. The pump features a compact structure, good self-priming ability, good efficiency, and strong adaptability.

The design considers interchangeability with the KCB series standard gear pump. Its external dimensions, installation dimensions, and technical parameters are described as fully aligned with the KCB series gear pump. For this reason, the ZYB-Z pump is also described as a reinforced type of the KCB series.

When transferring lubricating media without impurities, the pump supports stable pressure performance and long service life under suitable operating conditions.

## Features & Advantages

- **Impurity-Tolerant Heavy Oil Transfer:** Suitable for heavy oil, diesel oil, coal tar, and similar media containing non-metallic impurities.
- **Stable Continuous Operation:** Designed for applications requiring stable working pressure and long-term operation.
