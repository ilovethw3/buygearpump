The LRY vertical high-temperature thermal oil pump is mainly used for transferring high-temperature liquids with operating temperatures from -20°C to 350°C. It is suitable for hot oil transfer and other process applications requiring stable high-temperature liquid circulation.

The pump uses a soft packing seal with good thermal adaptability. It features good efficiency, compact size, low noise, and long-term stable operation under suitable working conditions. Different wetted-part materials can be selected for liquids with corrosive properties or special performance requirements.

## Structure & Working Features

The LRY vertical high-temperature thermal oil pump is a vertical centrifugal pump designed for special high-temperature operating conditions. The inlet and outlet are arranged horizontally, making pipeline connection convenient while saving installation space.

This pump is suitable for transferring thermal oil and other high-temperature liquids below 350°C. Its vertical structure helps reduce the floor area required for installation and supports process systems where compact layout is important.

## Features & Advantages

- **High-Temperature Liquid Transfer:** Suitable for thermal oil and similar high-temperature liquids from -20°C to 350°C.
- **Vertical Installation:** Compact vertical structure saves floor space.
- **Horizontal Pipeline Connection:** Horizontal inlet and outlet make piping easier to arrange.
- **Thermally Adaptable Seal:** Soft packing seal is used for high-temperature working conditions.
- **Material Selection Options:** Wetted parts can be selected according to corrosive or special media requirements.
- **Stable Operation:** Designed for low noise and long-term operation under suitable conditions.
