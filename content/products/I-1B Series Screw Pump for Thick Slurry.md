The I-1B series screw pump for thick slurry is a single-screw positive displacement rotary pump. It uses an eccentric single-helical screw rotating inside a double-helical liner to push thick slurry along the spiral groove from the suction port to the discharge port.

This pump is widely used in chemical, pharmaceutical, brewing, paper, food, and related industries. It is suitable for transferring thick slurry, high-viscosity liquids, suspended slurry, and particle-containing media under supported operating conditions.

The I-1B series screw pump provides stable flow with low pulsation, low agitation, and low shear. Its discharge pressure is not directly dependent on rotational speed, allowing the pump to maintain high discharge pressure even at low flow. Flow is proportional to speed and can be adjusted through a speed change mechanism or speed-regulating motor.

The pump has good self-priming capability and can directly draw liquid without a foot valve. It can also operate in reverse, allowing the liquid flow direction to change according to the pump rotation direction. This makes it suitable for pipeline systems that require forward and reverse flushing.

## Features & Advantages

- **High-Viscosity Slurry Transfer:** Suitable for thick slurry with viscosity below 10000 Pa·s and suspended media containing particles.
- **Stable Low-Shear Flow:** Transfers liquid smoothly with low pulsation, low agitation, and low shear effect.
- **Pressure Stability:** Discharge pressure is not directly dependent on speed, so high discharge pressure can be maintained at low flow.
- **Adjustable Flow:** Flow is proportional to pump speed and can be adjusted by a speed change mechanism or speed-regulating motor.
- **Self-Priming Operation:** Can directly draw liquid without installing a foot valve.
- **Reversible Flow Direction:** The liquid flow direction can be changed by reversing the pump rotation.
- **Smooth Operation:** Low vibration and low noise during operation.
- **Simple Maintenance:** Simple structure with convenient disassembly and repair.

## Structure & Material Options

The I-1B series screw pump is available in several structure and material configurations:

- **I-1BA Type:** Cast iron casing with general steel transmission components, suitable for general neutral thick slurry transfer.
- **I-1BB Type:** Stainless steel transmission components, including main shaft, screw, and flexible shaft, suitable for general slightly acidic or alkaline slurry transfer.
- **I-1BF Type:** Stainless steel transmission components and stainless steel pump casing in contact with slurry, suitable for food, pharmaceutical, and alkaline slurry transfer.

Rubber liner options include general stable-performance rubber, food-use rubber, and oil-resistant rubber according to user requirements.

## Drive Options

Available drive options include:

- Direct drive between motor and pump shaft
- Motor-driven pump through a reducer
- Motor-driven pump through V-belt pulley transmission

Motor options include general enclosed motors, stable-performance motors, and electromagnetic speed-regulating motors.
