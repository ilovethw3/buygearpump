The BCB cycloidal internal gear pump is suitable for transferring lubricating oil and other similar liquids that are non-corrosive and free of solid particles or fibers. It is designed for media with a temperature not higher than 150°C and viscosity from 5 to 1500 cSt.

This pump can be used as a transfer and booster pump in oil delivery systems, as a fuel pump for delivery, pressurization, and injection in fuel systems, and as a lubrication pump in industrial equipment and production systems.

## Working Principle & Structure

The BCB series gear pump mainly consists of gears, shaft, pump body, pump cover, and shaft-end sealing components. Its gear tooth profile uses a cycloidal arc design. The pump features a compact structure, smooth operation, good efficiency, and long service life. Seal options include mechanical seal and packing seal, which can be selected according to operating requirements.

## Features & Advantages

- **Suitable for Clean Oil Media:** Designed for lubricating oil and similar clean, non-corrosive liquids without particles or fibers.
- **Wide Viscosity Range:** Supports liquid viscosity from 5 to 1500 cSt.
- **Compact Pump Design:** Internal gear construction helps reduce installation space requirements.
- **Smooth and Stable Operation:** Cycloidal gear profile supports steady flow and reliable running.
- **Selectable Seal Types:** Mechanical seal and packing seal options are available for different working conditions.
- **Industrial Lubrication Use:** Suitable for oil transfer, fuel handling, and lubrication service.

## Typical Applications

- Oil transfer systems
- Oil booster systems
- Fuel delivery systems
- Fuel pressurization and injection systems
- Industrial lubrication systems
