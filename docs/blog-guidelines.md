# Blog Guidelines

Guidelines for writing and maintaining BuyGearPump blog articles.

## Audience

Write for overseas industrial buyers, engineers, equipment maintenance teams, and procurement staff who need practical information about gear pumps and industrial fluid handling.

## Tone

- Use professional English.
- Keep the writing clear, practical, and specific.
- Avoid exaggerated marketing claims.
- Do not invent certifications, stock status, test results, delivery times, prices, or technical parameters.
- Prefer straightforward wording over decorative language.

## File Format

Blog posts live in `content/blog/` as Markdown files.

Each post should include Hugo front matter:

```yaml
---
title: "Article Title"
slug: "article-title"
date: 2026-05-12T10:00:00+08:00
draft: false
description: "A concise SEO description for the article."
image: "images/blog/example.jpg"
author: "Tyler"
---
```

Use lowercase hyphenated slugs. Keep descriptions concise and useful for search results.

## SEO Structure

- Assign one primary keyword per article.
- Use two to four related secondary keywords when natural.
- Include the primary keyword in the title or first paragraph when it reads naturally.
- Use clear H2 and H3 headings.
- Add internal links to relevant product, FAQ, contact, or related blog pages when useful.
- End with a practical conclusion or inquiry-oriented call to action.

## Article Structure

A typical article should include:

- A short introduction that states the problem or question.
- Practical sections organized by selection, installation, maintenance, troubleshooting, applications, or comparison.
- Specific details that help the reader make a decision.
- A concise conclusion with a natural path to contact BuyGearPump.

## Blog Cover Images

- AI-generated images may be used for blog cover images.
- Blog cover images should be prepared at **1740 × 904 px**.
- Generate abstract cover images based on the article topic and industrial context.
- Keep blog covers simple and low-detail to reduce visual errors.
- Avoid text, labels, gauges, readable markings, complex machinery details, or realistic product-specific claims in generated covers.
- Do not use AI-generated images as product photos or as evidence of actual equipment appearance.
- Product pages should use real product images whenever possible.

## Encoding

Use UTF-8 for all blog files. Non-ASCII punctuation is allowed when appropriate, but plain ASCII punctuation is also acceptable for consistency.
